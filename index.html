1 The team

Each person has a colour, taken from the sticky notes they used on the board. Everything you write on this site is tagged with your name and your colour, so the rest of the team always knows who said what.

How to use this site: click Pick your name in the top-right, choose yourself, and then click the 💬 button under any note, project or section to add a note or a question. Your notes show up in your colour, here and in the All Notes feed.

2 Sharing notes with the team

Once sharing is switched on, there is nothing for you to set up: open the link, pick your name, and you see everyone's notes. New notes from the rest of the team appear on their own, without reloading.

For everyone on the team

  1. Open the board link.
  2. Click Pick your name and choose yourself.
  3. Write notes. That is all — no account, no sign-in, nothing to install.

Switching sharing on (once, by one person)

Create a free Supabase project, run the small SQL snippet, and paste the project URL and anon key into assets/config.js. Full step-by-step instructions are written at the top of that file.

Until that is done, notes stay in each person's own browser and a warning strip shows at the top of every page.

If the connection drops

Nothing is lost. Notes written while offline are kept in your browser, marked not synced, and sent automatically once the board can reach the server again.
