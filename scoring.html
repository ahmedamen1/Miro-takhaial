Score & Choose a Project

Score each project 0 to 10 on every criterion — plain numbers, so the maths is easy to check by hand. Each criterion carries a weight, also 0 to 10. The total is the weighted average, and the matrix below plots every project live and downloads with all the titles on it.

Criteria & weights Score the projects Ranking Importance matrix Open one project
How the number works: every criterion is scored 0–10 where 10 is always good. So for Low cost / low risk and Fast to deliver, 10 means cheap and quick, not expensive and slow. Final score = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ(weight), which keeps it on the same 0–10 scale. Click any project name to open its own page.
